## Employee Salary Management System Requirements
---

## 

## \*\*Goal\*\*

## Enable HR managers to manage salary data for 10,000 employees across multiple countries through a web-based system, replacing error-prone Excel workflows.

## 

## \*\*Scope \& Features\*\*

## \- Employee CRUD

## \- Bulk operations

## \- Search \& filters

## \- Audit logging

## \- Analytics

## \- Access control

## 

## \*\*Deliberately Excluded\*\*

## \- Payroll integration

## \- Complex benefits/deductions

## \- Multi-currency conversions
